>[!Physical Domains]
Physical energy focuses on magic and abilities that relate to the matter and elements that make up the material realm. Additionally, dragons that primarily fall under the physical domains feel most comfortable and have an easier time existing on Aeos. Auras based in the physical domains are often detectable by smell or taste.
Earth: Lawful Good
Abilities: solids, metal, minerals, magma, tectonic
Aura Sign: metallic/oily
Water: Chaotic Good
Abilities: ice, acid, water, vapor, liquids, tides
Aura Sign: petrichor
Air: Chaotic Evil
Abilities: weather, smoke, gas, vacuums, atmosphere
Aura Sign: ozone
Fire: Lawful Evil
Abilities: fire, heat, plasma, combustion, stars
Aura Sign: smokey

>[!Metaphysical Domains]
Metaphysical energy is not purely non-physical abilities, but each domain does center around a more nebulous non-concrete idea. These dragons usually have an easier time possessing living creatures due to their vaporous nature. Auras in these domains often trick the brain and overload neurons that try to pick up on them.
Spirit: Neutral Good
Abilities: mind, soul, consciousness, memory, thought
Aura Sign: headaches
Blood: Neutral Evil
Abilities: living matter, life energy, bonds, genetics
Aura Sign: body aches
Shadow: Chaotic Neutral
Abilities: darkness, reflections, stealth, absence
Aura Sign: sensory deprivation
Light: Lawful Neutral
Abilities: light, color, illusion, perception
Aura Sign: sensory overload
