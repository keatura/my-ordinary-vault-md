**Chantmaster Zekka** is a Goblin Shaman, and the leader of the goblin tribe on Kalivny. She is very old for a goblin, the ground below her literally molds as she wants it

#### Stats
Small Humanoid  
AC: 14 (mage armor)
HP: 38 (5d8+10)
Speed: 30ft  
**STR: 10(0), DEX: 12(+1), CON: 15(+2), INT: 12(+1), WIS: 17(+3), CHA: 11(0)**  
Skills: Stealth +7, Perception +6, Nature +4
Senses: Darkvision 60ft, Passive perception 16
CR 1/4 PB +3

**Spellcasting.** The Goblin Shaman is a 4th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following druid spells prepared:

Cantrips (at will): druidcraft, produce flame, shillelagh, frost bolt, mage armor

1st level (4 slots): entangle, longstrider, speak with animals, thunderwave

2nd level (3 slots): animal messenger, enlarge/reduce

3rd level (2 slots): conjure animals, wind wall

**Airwalker.** Chantmaster Zekka can walk on air. Because of this, her footsteps are silent, and she has a fly speed of 30ft.

**Actions.**

***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit (+6 to hit with *shillelagh*), reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage, 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage if wielded with two hands, or 6 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage with *shillelagh*.

***Frost Bolt.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 60ft., one target. Hit: 4 (2d8) cold damage, and the target has disadvantage on the next weapon attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

**Bonus Actions.**

**Nimble Escape.**
*The Goblin can take the disengage or hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.*

**Reactions.**

***Vine Shield.*** When any creature is targeted with an attack within 30ft of the Goblin Shaman, this creature may use it's reaction to give the target +2 AC until the start of their next turn. The effected creature may not move under any circumstance until the start of their next turn.
