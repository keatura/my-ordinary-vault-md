***Restrictive Armor.*** The armor this creature is wearing restricts it's movement, but not it's ability to aim. This creature's Dexterity score counts as 18(+4) when determining ranged weapon attacks.
***Powered Armor.*** The armor this creature is wearing boosts it's Strength Score from 22 to 30, gives it resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, and makes it's wearer immune to effects of poison gas and other chemical weapons, and magical effects such as Cloudkill. If this creatures armor is removed, it loses these traits.
***Kadis' Chosen.*** (3/day) When the Templar fails a roll, it may choose to succeed instead.
***Shock Absorbers.*** The Templar is immune to fall damage.

***Aura of Protection.*** Maximus and his allies within 30 feet have a bonus to their saving throws equal to his Charisma modifier. 

***Angelic Ascension.*** (Recharge 1d4 weeks.) When reduced to 0 hit-points, Maximus channels the raw power of the pantheon flowing through him, surrounding himself with divine energy and radiance and unfurling massive spectral wings from behind him. His size grows to Huge, He gains a flying speed of 120ft, his HP is set to 600, he gains access to his Mythic Actions, and he loses 10hp at the start of each of his turns. If Maximus is reduced to zero by a hostile attacker during this state, he permanently dies. If he is reduced to zero by this ability, he enters a state of torpor that lasts until the ability recharges. If Maximus takes any damage during this state of torpor he instantly dies.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Maximus' innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (Spell Save DC 23, +15 to hit with Spell Attacks). 

At Will: *Light*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Zone of Truth*, *Heroism (3rd level)*
3/day each: *Lesser Restoration*, *Gentle Repose*, *Purify Food and Drink*
1/day: *Summon Celestial*, *Destructive Wave*

**Actions.**
***Multiattack.*** Maximus makes two attacks with his *Shoulder-mounted Autocannon*, and three attacks with his sword or autorifle.

***Shoulder-mounted Autocannon.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, range (200/600)., Hit: 18 (4d6 + 4) piercing damage. Plus 1d10 piercing damage to the target creatures within 10ft.

***Sword of Maximus. (+3)*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +17 to hit, reach 10ft., Hit: 21 (3d6 + 11) slashing damage. Plus 14 (3d8) radiant damage. If the target is a *Fiend or Undead*, they take an additional 14 (3d8) radiant damage.

***Maximus' Autorifle. (+2)*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach (300/900)., Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage.

***Frightening Prescence.*** As an action, all hostile creatures within 120ft of Maximus must make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for the next minute. A frightened creature's speed is reduced to 0 and they drop any items they are holding. A creature may remake this saving throw at the end of each of their turns. If a creature passes this save, it is immune to the effects of this ability for the next 24 hours.

**Bonus Actions.**

***Lay on Hands.*** (150hp, recharges on a Long Rest) Maximus' blessings allow him to heal those by channeling healing magic from a reserved pool through his palms. As a bonus action, He may touch a willing creature (including himself) and heal them, drawing the health given from the pool.
