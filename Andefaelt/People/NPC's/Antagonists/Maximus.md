- Ancient Aasimar of Yorinth, lives with the sole duty of "preserving the law and order in the plane."

Of all the Templars of the Asyrian Church, one stands above all. Maximus. An Aasimar of Yorinth who's life is dedicated to preserving law and order within the plane, he has one sole goal: crush any and all "evil-doers." While just being an Aasimar as old as he is an achievement in of itself, he is the most accomplished soldier, possibly in the history of Andefaelt. Fitting his name, Maximus always puts his full effort forward into any task he sets his mind to, and has always been a man of strong morals and a force of personality to match his immense strength. Currently, while acting with the Asyrian Church, he has a certain level of autonomy from the Archbishop, due to his Blessing of Kadis. This makes him a sort of "Hybrid" Aasimar, originally created as Yorinth's first aspect, then blessed by Kadis to fulfill his duty successfully. Maximus has fought in countless battles over the years, but the most important is the one upon which he received his blessing, in 1366. Doing battle with a cult using Iodrosni's image as a masquerade, He single-handedly fought off waves of cultists and their summoned devils and fiends. During the battle he was brought to his knees by a Greater Devil, and in that moment he gave a prayer to the gods. Upon this moment, a radiant light proceeded to burst out from his soul, causing him to simultaneously earn his wings and grow significantly in size. After he the light finished shining, Maximus stood several feet above his foes, and struck them down one-by-one with his bare hands.

## Lore of Maximus.
---
##### 4.17.1366
Maximus earned his notoriety during the Battle of Aurora's Keep, a devastating battle for Asyria after they had just finished they conquest of the Central Islands. Especially Aurora's Keep, an old Iyamarian Keep that was being converted into a fortress for the Asyrian Empire. Unfortunately, the Children of Iodrosni had a heavy presence on the island, and with the instability caused by the conflict they were taking full leverage of their chance to expand their influence. This all culminated in an assault on Aurora's Keep, while Asyrian forces were still recovering inside from the war that had just ended. Then finally on a "bright" and early on a Thursday morning, a torrential downpour and thunder overhead, as only nine templars and several artillery crews stared down a horde of daemons and cultists, High-Templar Maximus stood at the head of his formation.

The Battle initially began smoothly, The black powder cannons openly firing into the horde, taking dozens with each blast. But they couldn't fire fast enough, even working full speed the rain hindered the crews ability to reload the cannons, and many misfires occurred due to the rain wetting the gunpowder. But soon, the tide reached the gates, the Templars standing in a shield wall with their pikes and steam-powered armor, Maximus at the head. Managing to catch many foe each on each of their near 20-foot long polearms, the Templars held the gates, but eight men can only hold off thousands for so long. Eventually, overwhelmed by musket fire and minor daemons scratching at their shields, One of the Templars fell due to sheer exhaustion, causing the line to break. The enemy then proceeded to flood in, and pierce the weak points of the fallen Templars armor, killing him. This left eight. The demons swarming in and cutting through the artillery crews as the Devils bombarded the fort with fiendish spells.

The Templars quickly fell back, their line splitting. Though, Maximus, under heavy assault, successfully guided his remaining men. Taking 3 of the seven others with him, and assigning the other four to guard the barracks and protect the wounded, Maximus grabbed a loaded cannon with the help of his three brothers, and aimed it towards one of the three devils leading the assault, blowing a hole through the devil's chest and killing it. Throwing the heavy cannon into a large group of assaulting demons, scattering them, the Templars jumped down from the wall into the courtyard of the fortress. Fighting their way through the tide of demons towards their brothers and sisters defending the barracks, tearing through them with swords and even their bare hands, they reached what was a very grim sight. Two of the templars had fallen and been killed, and the other two had been pushed back into the barracks, with demons swarming in and killing the injured soldiers inside. 

Enraged, Maximus led a charge with his brothers to a very gruesome sight, daemons and minor devils feasting on the blood and guts of his regiment throughout the halls of the fort. Maximus began tearing through the horde, ripping off a large demons' horns and using them as knives after his sword was blunted and broken. Eventually, the force inside the barracks was repelled, but three more templars were lost in the process. Alas, the battle was reaching it's close. Maximus and his two remaining brothers returned out to the courtyard, Maximus witnessed a horrific sight. The two remaining Devils had summoned a Greater Daemon in the center of the fort, a towering beast standing nearly four times taller than Maximus. Minotaur-like, with pointed horns and clawed hands that glinted like steel, it let out a mighty roar. The two Devils fell back, allowing the Greater Daemon to support the small amount of their remaining forces, and then it snorted and charged Maximus.

The force of the blow sent Maximus flying into the back wall of the fort, concussing him and destroying his armor's engine. Watching through dazed eyes, Maximus watched his two remaining brothers be overrun and gored by this great beast, all the while being stricken by magical blasts from the Devils. Slowly recovering, Maximus ejected the back-plate of his now wrecked armor, Maximus rallied himself, turning to face his foes, covered mud and blood, holding nothing but his broken sword. Maximus stared down his inevitable fate, and decreed, bellowing out for all to hear, "EVEN IN VICTORY, YOUR REIGN SHALL NOT LAST. I, MAXIMUS STAND ALONE, ETERNALLY YOUR FOE, AND IF I AM TO DIE HERE, MAY MY MEMORY HAUNT YOUR KIN FOR MILLENIA TO COME." 

As Maximus prepared himself for his last stand, he charged forward, slashing through daemons with his broken sword until he was surrounded and face-to-face with the Beast. With a wide swing, Maximus was trapped underneath of this beasts' hands, his head sticking out between two of its claws.
