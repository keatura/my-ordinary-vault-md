Taegen is the second-newest member of Glass One, and a major know-it-all. He graduated top of his classes throughout his education, and has put it to work in the field with the IAR, investigating the interests of the nation. ***Predictive Defense.*** Taegen may substitute his Intelligence for his Dexterity when determining his Armor Class, and add his Intelligence to his Initiative. (+5)

###### Actions.

***Military Rifle.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack.* +4 to hit, reach 200/600ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 1) piercing damage. Reload(5).

***First-Aid Training.*** Taegen attempts to quickly bandage a wound on himself or a willing creature within 5 feet. Taegen expends one charge of his First-Aid Kit and heals the target for 2d8+4. (4/day)

***Tinker.*** When Taegen is within 5ft of a construct or vehicle, he may attempt to repair it. He rolls 1d8 + 4, and the target regains the rolled amount of HP. (4/day)

###### Bonus Actions.

***Study Target.*** Taegen takes a few seconds to calmly study a target and predict their next moves. Taegen may give himself advantage on his next attack against a creature until the end of his turn.
