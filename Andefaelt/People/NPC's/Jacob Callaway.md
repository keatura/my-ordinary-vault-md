**Jacob Callaway** is a dwarven man, and the Captain of the City Guard in [[Kalivny]]. He is roughly 80, about halfway through his life. He is a gruff man, and very busy as he has had to manage the caravans between Helena and Kalivny and with the increase in goblin activity Helena is threatening to cut trade ties.
