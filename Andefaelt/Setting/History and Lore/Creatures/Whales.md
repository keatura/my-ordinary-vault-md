Whales in Andefaelt are giant, bulbous flying creatures that generally hang close to the blanket, feasting on flocks of birds and other animals that they can catch. Though, it is noted that despite their huge size and mass, they are able to "swim" through the air with their powerful webbed tails. Whales stay afloat through the other important part of their diet, Moxite crystals. Whales are known to eat these crystals as they grow along the bottoms of islands, both creating new space for these crystals to grow, and as they dissolve in the whales' stomach, they turn into a similar compound as what is used in modern Moxite Lifting Devices. Featuring two pairs of large fins (wingspan of 30-45 feet in grown Adults) along their pectoral muscles and midsection. Their dorsal fins are large (6-10 feet) and whales have several along the top of their body and their belly and tail. with their tail fins placed vertically, and measured from top to bottom range from 12 to 25 feet. They have 3 eyes on each side, aligned in a row that are able to act independently and spot potential prey from miles away. 

These whales are known to be intelligent, sometimes pranking ships by bumping them or flying incredibly close to their superstructures and mate for life, travelling in pairs with their partner and children. They have been noted to use the winds from Ederna to reduce the effort of flying by using it to "slingshot" themselves long distances with minimal effort. Because of this nature, they have been attacked out of both fear, self defense, and are hunted for sport and money, as their stomach acid capable of digesting Moxite makes for a Very Good Acid, melting through nearly every kind of metal and common construction material, as well as crystalline structures.
