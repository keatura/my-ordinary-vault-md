Pegasi (Pegasus plural) are a myth and legend in Andefaelt. The concept of a magical horse with wings is a seemingly unbelievable concept, yet one that still lives the in the minds of many children. While none are currently in captivity, and as of modern times it is unclear whether they actually still exist, many tales surround these creature with stories of adventure and heroism. One such case is the children's story is a tale that was written in the 1680's by an Iyamarian Author called My Flying Horsey: Friendship Teaches You Magic. This story is famous for teaching many many kids about the various mythos of the Pegasus through the main character Niko and her Pegasus friend Theodore travelling the plane and meeting new people. They are rumored to exist on the fringes of society, having moved to islands yet-to-be-explored, though no proof has been shown.
