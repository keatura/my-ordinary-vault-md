Goblins are inherently intwined with nature, and are related to plants. It is rumored that they were created when Hemepha, seeing her older sibling's creation of life and nature in the world, wanted to do this for herself, and put hearts and minds into her plants, creating the goblins. The first druidic orders were comprised of the more spiritual goblins, and Hobgoblins are "Protectors" of life in Andefaelt, acting as Knights in goblin society.

Their worship of Hemepha is also the reason why they choose to isolate themselves from society, despite their ability to conform. Occasionally, there will be goblins that exist within society, but most often they are isolationist tribes who live in the wilds. They dislike the constriction of laws, as their deep worship of nature also deepends their chaotic nature.
