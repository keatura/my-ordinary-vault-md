

Aasimar color depends on their origin - 
- Aasimar chosen by Kadis tend to have silver-like skin with black marbling.
- Aasimar chosen by Kaine tend to be a golden yellow.
- Aasimar chosen by Hemepha tend to be dark green or purple.
- Aasimar chosen by Yorinth tend to appear a dark-oak color for law, and a dark gray cold iron color for order.
