The capital city of Zevka, Zhizni-Mayutsya was the original city-state of Zevka. It has since been re-named to it's current title meaning something roughly along the lines of "Mother's Warm(comforting?) Embrace." This city is home to Fortress Silvanus, the castle and royal residence of the Silvain bloodline and rulers of Zevka. Otherwise, the capital is a bustling center of almost everything Zevka does well. Skilled workers and artists produce finely made traditional garments, and have a history of their tapestries being bought and commissioned by many other previous rulers and nobles of old.
