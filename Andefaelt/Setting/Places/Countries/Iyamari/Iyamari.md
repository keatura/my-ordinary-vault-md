Iyamari is to the southeast of Ederna, and is the homeland of Elves. also called the Great Plains, the southeast of Andefaelt where the Iyamari live is a massive span of anywhere between fertile plains and forested islands. The Iyamari are a more scattered people, with many islands lacking major cities and being made up of hundreds of villages that intertwine together. Individual community is important in Iyamari with individual villages functioning as separate societies, with interlocking trade, feuds, and sometimes even skirmishes. There is a Capital city and government of Iyamari, but it mainly serves as a mediating force for the villages, and makes sure any skirmishes do not cause civil war in the country.

Iyamari's council is run by 13 elected members who serve for 13 years. Every year, one council member is replaced by a new one who is voted in. This election generally occurs through the months of August and September, and then the new Councilor is properly seated in office by the end of the year. Votes in specific buildings build by the Iyamarian Court of Sorcerers, and are enchanted to recognize specific people and not allow them back in after they vote. After the votes are cast and they are sent on mail carts and trucks to the capital, where they are counted by the same Court. 
- This has lead to accusations of corruption before by small groups of upset parties, but nothing has ever been proven after intense investigations and surveillance. 
- As well, Iyamarian Councilors are only allowed to serve one term in their life, even Elves don't get multiple.
