While less common than guns, Grenades and other expendables are a prevalent part of mercenary and military tactics in Andefaelt. Combining Runesteel with explosives and other chemicals to create unique effects. Grenades are harder to get than guns, but still mostly available to the public as long as they are non-fragmentation. Smoke grenades, hunting flashbangs, and with the proper licenses (or a less-than-legal alternative) things like dynamite and other quarrying explosives.

Most grenades will be found inside of hunting stores, gun stores as a secondary stock, or the black market. Openly carrying grenades of any type is generally frowned upon, but soldiers are a common sight in Andefaelt and if you do a good impression, you can get away with it.

There are two ways to use a grenade, and they correspond with each grenades two range profiles;
