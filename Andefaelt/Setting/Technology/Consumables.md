While less common than guns, Grenades and other expendables are a prevalent part of mercenary and military tactics in Andefaelt. Combining Runesteel with explosives and other chemicals to create unique effects. Grenades are harder to get than guns, but still mostly available to the public as long as they are non-fragmentation. Smoke grenades, hunting flashbangs, and with the proper licenses (or a less-than-legal alternative) things like dynamite and other quarrying explosives. Although, buying illegally can lead to inconsistent and expensive pricing.

Most grenades will be found inside of hunting stores, gun stores as a secondary stock, or the black market. Openly carrying grenades of any type is generally frowned upon, but soldiers are a common sight in Andefaelt and if you do a good impression, you can get away with it.

There are two ways to use a grenade, and they correspond with each grenades two range profiles; neither profile may target a creature specifically. The throwing creature selects a specific space within range for the grenade to land, where it then causes it's effect. Although, some grenades may specify requiring an attack roll, where Dexterity is used and it is treated as an Improvised Weapon.

Firstly you may **Underhand Toss** a grenade. This involves a light toss, or even just rolling it along the floor. an Underhand Toss takes a Bonus Action, uses the first range profile of a grenade, and does not end the Hidden condition when used.

You may also **Overhand Toss** a grenade. this involves a full throwing arm, and allows for much greater range. an Overhand Toss takes an Action (and counts as a bonus action for Thief Rogues item interaction, uses the second range profile of a grenade, and ends the Hidden condition when used.
