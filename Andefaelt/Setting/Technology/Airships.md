Airships in Andefaelt are used for almost everything related to inter-island travel. Whether it be shipping, warfare, transport or anything else under the sun of large scale movement. Airship design varies heavily between different nations and manufacturers, and doctrine heavily influences the different nations navies. Large scale civilian cargo ships generally follow a simple boxy design, needing only simple aerodynamics and bridges, while having a large square section for carrying containers.

Airship propulsion has undergone much development in Andefaelt, but there are three distinct eras.

- The Age of Sail

- The Age of Propellers

- The Age of Turbines (current era)

Naming conventions for ships in Andefaelt is very dependent on the nation it is from. First of all, civilian ships generally do not have prefixes. So names may consist of simple names like "Constellation" and "Beaver", though all ships are required to fly large flags of their origin on their mast. Meanwhile, ships owned by a country or flying under a military are given a prefix for identification;
- City-States do not generally give named prefixes, partially because their navies are generally small to non-existent, and partially because their militias and minor militaries are not deemed important or internationally recognized, if they have one at all. There is one exception to this:

- Voxis, the largest and richest of all of the City-States, has it's own standing navy that while small compared to nations, is gigantic compared to their peers. While it is mainly used for escorting trade ships to dock and making sure pirates do not raid anything within the general area of their island, they do have standing warships for this purpose, purchases from various countries or gained in trade agreements.
	- Voxan Airship Prefix is: Voxis Naval Defense Brigade (VNDB)

- Asyria has the largest and most powerful standing navy in Andefaelt. They parade around the borders of Asyria frequently showing off their military might and also spotless with a fresh coat of paint. One of the biggest points of pride in Asyria is the navy, and they having leading edge tech in the world of naval combat, only second to Onyxia. Their ships are heavy armed and armored, and follow a doctrine of overwhelming firepower and focus super heavily on superb crew training. Asyrian ships are shaped like traditional zeppelins, with armor, superstructure along the top and the keel, and guns bristling along all sides, all completed with a large turbine unit positioned at the rear. Although, Asyria can only maintain this fleet with their dominant position in the world, they are and even then it is being pushed between the navy and the extreme expense of building Solata.
	- Asyrian Airship Prefix is: Imperial Asyrian Airship (IAA)

- Iyamari has the most unique ship design of the four nations. As they were the inventors of the airplane and their huge swathes of flat land and high winds leading to lots of development in airfoil technology. Iyamarian ships are generally shaped like huge flying wings, allowing them to gain great altitude while they are maintaining forward flight, much higher than every other nations airship. This has lead to development placing almost the entirety of the ships armor and artillery on the underside, with only small view cabins for gun direction and something only seen on Iyamarian ship design -- Landing gear bays. While Iyamari has started to enter the age of Turbines with their ships, they greatly expanding their navy during the age of Propellers and most of their infrastructure is designed towards maintaining those, and not turbines. Modernizing will take a considerable amount of time or urgency.
	- Iyamarian Airship Prefix is: Peoples' Council Airship (PCA) for non-combat vessels and Peoples' Council Warship (PCW) for combat vessels.

- Onyxian Airship design is more about experimentation that a cohesive navy. They build new vessels that always contain the newest tech invented in Noraveas and are always expensive and go over budget. While this isn't to say that the Onyxian Navy isn't competent, it has lead to a stigma within the people and a joke to those outside Onyxia. Onyxian Airships are in theory the best of all worlds: Heavy armor, long range and accurate artillery, and advanced systems for aiming the guns and defenses all topped off with powerful engines for every era, because Noraveas is the home of the turbine engine. Though these advanced ships referred to as "Flying Castles" (Darazgund) in Onyxia are of course, Very Expensive. Onyxian ship design takes the name very literally. In many instances featuring very boxy and geometric outwards design, and often incorporating motifs of actual castles such as casemated secondary guns protected by heavy armor, superstructure shaped like a castle keep with towers and battlements on the railings. Ships that are not classified as Darazgund are not given prefixes. One of their most unique inventions and innovations in naval combat is how they board enemy ships. Their Darazgund have angled spinal-mounted cannons that fire "Battle Balls" (Gabil-Du). Armored balls that have thrusters and extending wing to pierce enemy armor with extreme precision via a large drill mounted on the front. When the ball then lands inside the ship, it opens, allowing Dwarven Marines to enter and wreak havoc.
	- Onyxian Airship Prefix is: Onyxian Darazgund (OD)

- Zevka follows a very iconic design method; Keep It Simple, Stupid. Their ships are basic, easy to manufacture and maintain, and consistent. While they dont have high end tech or giant wings, they have boats that work, boats that are cheap, and boats that still hit just as hard. Even with all of this, Zevkan ships still have one big capability advantage: altitude. while of course not as extreme as Iyamari flying wings that soar miles above their target, they must be capable enough to have regular operation at the higher altitudes of Zevka. Their ships are basic, following similar principles and Asyrian ship design but featuring large Moxite lifting devices compared for their size, making them volatile when the armor is pierced. As well, These simple principles do lead to less capable ships compared to other nations, especially when determining targeting and speed.
	- Zevkan Airship Prefix is: (the) Kings Navy Ship (KNS)

Due to being flying vessels, Airships need safety precautions when crew members step outside. While most civilian ships will have fences around any exterior area so crew members cannot get shaken and tossed overboard, but sometimes the exterior plating of the ship needs to be accessed. To access these, every ship in the modern era is equipped with a system of rails on the exterior of the ship, and crew members wear harnesses with rope clipped to these rails. to go along with this, magnetic runesteel gloves and shoes have started to enter service, allowing crew members to more easily move around on the outside of a ship, and even work while the ship is moving.

"Shipwrights" are small 4' by 4' hexapod 'Jacks that are present on almost every single modern Airship. They are small battery-powered 'Jacks that can automatically repair exterior damage to airships to reduce the loss of life from EVA (Extravehicular Activity) and allow for crew to be reassigned to other places. These bots generally have a specific compartment where they stay and recharge from the ships engine. On larger ships these robots even have their own maintenance tunnels, where they can navigate the whole ship, and be assigned to manage smaller systems and even reload the ships artillery. While their design varies from nation to nation, the concept is the same between all of them, without much room for change. the Hexapod design was chosen for maximum magnetic grip while still allowing an efficient and quick speed.
