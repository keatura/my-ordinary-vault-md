## Characters

[Mizuki Kotova](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Oikd89fGU7T00rWWaWLT19Nso57PAbQDarz4r-o64E8/edit?usp=sharing) - An adopted child of the high-class Iyamarian family, the Hayashi's. After a venture into the woods gone wrong, she lost everything and moved to Zevka, searching for purpose and security.

[Sascha King](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YRq8hfhHdB6ZylcPvlW80G39SPa8ycdb-BgVQJyk2YU/edit?usp=sharing) - Is a experienced Iyamarian Hammer agent, born to a high-ranking and accomplished father. Though, after he was sent on a mission regarding a very dangerous prototype weapon made by Asyria, he has started questioning his loyalty.

[[Hearsay]] - A Tiefling on her proving from Is'Ohenna, who has a great amount of expectations to live up to. 

[[Ezaren Reed]] - A very green Iyamarian Glass Agent, who is hoping to eventually find his long-lost mother.

#### Recap:
Last Session, after running away from a large engagement in a very small Goblin Camp, you all found yourself face-to-face with some Iyamarian Agents. Turns out, Ezaren wasn't just a normal mercenary! He was actually an Iyamarian Glass Agent working with at least two very experienced co-workers, Koran and Mako who were supposedly looking for Black Mamba. After a surprise Goblin ambush allowed three of you to escape, you fled back to Kalivny looking for rest and recovery, and finally meeting up with your friends after some days of chaos. Black Mamba and Mizuki spent time in the tavern sharing stories, filling their bellies and meeting the... eccentric tavern host Fenris. Hearsay allowed the party to lay their eyes upon both Bolt and Bones, her roommate and apparently Ukuchan best friend Bolt. And of course, a work-in-progress Warjack being kept literally under wraps.
